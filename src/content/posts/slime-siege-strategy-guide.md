Slime Siege looks simple: your sword fires on its own and you only steer. But
the difference between a 3,000-point run and a 30,000-point run is mostly
knowledge. This guide covers everything the game doesn't tell you, using the
real numbers from the game's code.

## The basics that matter most

**You never need to press attack.** Auto-fire is on by default, so your only
job is positioning. On a phone, drag anywhere on the screen. Your hero moves
with your thumb, so you don't have to cover him with your finger. If you prefer
to fire by hand, press **F** to turn auto-fire off and hold **Space**.

**You can't go to the top of the screen.** The hero is limited to roughly the
lower two-thirds of the play area. That's on purpose: enemies need room to
appear. Use the full width, though. The edges are often the safest place when
a boss fires a fan of shots down the middle.

**You have three hearts and can hold up to five.** After you're hit you flash
for two seconds and can't be hurt again. Any enemy shots close to you also
vanish when you take a hit, so a hit never chains into a second one.

## Know your enemies

| Enemy | Hits to kill (first loop) | Points | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slime | 2 | 10 | Hops slowly down the path towards you. |
| Skeleton | 4 | 25 | Walks down, drifts towards your side of the path and throws bones aimed at you. |
| Big slime | 8 | 40 | Slow and tough. Only appears in the Haunted Wood. |

Two things follow from this table:

1. **Skeletons are the real threat.** Slimes only hurt you if you walk into
   them. Skeletons shoot at you. When a skeleton appears, move so it's in line
   with your sword and kill it first. Skeletons slowly drift towards your
   column anyway, which makes them easy to line up.
2. **Bones are aimed, not homing.** A skeleton throws at the spot you're
   standing on when it throws. A small sidestep right after the throw is enough
   to dodge. Don't make big panicky moves, because that's how you run into a
   slime.

Enemies don't drop items very often: a slime about 1 time in 25, a skeleton
about 1 in 8. Big slimes drop something half of the time, so they're worth
the extra hits.

## Chests are your best friend

A treasure chest rolls down the path at a regular interval, about every
twelve seconds of play. Hit it three times and it pops open with **two**
items. Always break chests. Steer into line with one as soon as you see it.

What can come out of a chest (and from enemy drops):

| Item | Chance | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carrot | 30% | +1 heart (up to 5). At 5 hearts it's worth 100 points instead. |
| Gold | 25% | +100 points. |
| Crystal | 20% | **Triple slash** for 10 seconds: three slashes in a fan. |
| Swift boots | 15% | **Swift slash** for 10 seconds: you attack about two-thirds faster. |
| Bloom flower | 10% | +1 bloom bomb (you can carry up to 3). |

Only one power-up is active at a time. Picking up boots while you have the
crystal replaces triple slash with swift slash, and the timer restarts. As a
rule of thumb, triple slash is better against crowds of slimes and swift slash
is better against bosses, because all of its damage goes into one target.

## Bloom bombs: when to use them

You start every stage with at least one bloom bomb, and any you didn't use
carry over to the next stage. Press **X**, **K**, **Shift** or the BLOOM
button on a phone to set one off. A bloom:

- wipes **every enemy shot** off the screen,
- deals 3 damage to every enemy (enough to kill any slime outright),
- deals 10 damage to the boss.

The mistake most players make is saving bombs "for later" and dying with three
of them. A good habit: **use a bloom the moment you're about to take a hit you
can't dodge.** A bloom is worth less than a heart, and since you get at least
one back every stage, they're meant to be spent.

## Save the animals (and don't hit them)

Chickens, pigs, sheep and cows wander across the field. Walk into one to send
it home: **+50 points** each, which is more than five slimes. They're the
easiest points in the game.

But if one of your slashes hits an animal, you **lose 20 points** and the
startled animal bolts at triple speed. You can't
save it while it's panicking, and it usually leaves the screen before it
calms down. When an animal crosses in front of
you, shift sideways for a moment so your slashes miss it, then go and collect
it.

## The three stages

1. **Green Meadow.** Mostly slimes, with the odd skeleton. Use this stage to
   collect animals and open every chest, because it's the safest place to bank
   points and hearts.
2. **Old Farm.** Skeletons become common. Stay near the middle so you can
   reach either side quickly, and kill skeletons before they get
   close enough that their bones are hard to dodge.
3. **Haunted Wood.** Big slimes join in. Don't let them reach the bottom of
   the screen: start hitting them as early as you can, and use the triple
   slash if you have it.

When you clear all three, the run **loops** back to the Green Meadow with
everything tougher: enemies have 50% more health per loop, shoot faster
bullets and come in bigger waves. Bosses are worth more points on every loop
too.

## Boss 1: the King Slime

The King Slime sways left and right at the top of the screen and cycles
through three attacks, each lasting about four seconds:

- **Fan shot:** five slime balls in a wide fan. Stand at the edge of the fan,
  or go between two shots. The gaps are bigger the further down you are.
- **Spawn:** it drops small slimes. Kill them quickly so they don't block your
  slashes.
- **Aimed shot:** a single fast ball aimed at you. Sidestep once.

Because the King Slime moves side to side, the best plan is to **follow it**,
staying below and slightly to one side of it. Every slash that lands also
gives you a point, so long boss fights aren't wasted time.

## Boss 2: the Bone Lord

The Bone Lord, at the end of the Old Farm, is the hardest moment of the first
loop:

- **Triple aimed shot:** three bones in a tight spread at your position. Move
  sideways a good distance, not just a step.
- **Spinning ring:** eight bones fired in a ring that turns each time. This is
  the attack that ends most runs. Stay low on the screen, where the gaps
  between bones are widest, and slip through a gap rather than trying to
  outrun the ring. If you're not confident, this is the moment to use a bloom.
- **Summon:** a skeleton walks in from one side. Kill it before it starts
  throwing, or you'll have two sources of bones at once.

## Quick checklist for a high score

- Open every chest.
- Save every animal, and never slash one.
- Kill skeletons first, slimes second.
- Spend bloom bombs to avoid hits; don't hoard them.
- Use swift slash on bosses and triple slash on crowds.
- Bank hearts in the Green Meadow before the loop gets harder.

Your best score is saved on your device, and if you've set a player name it
goes on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard/) too. Good luck, and watch out for
that ring of bones.
