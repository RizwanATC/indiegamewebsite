[Pond Guard](/games/pond-guard/) starts gently: a few ants, a pile of coins
and a lot of empty grass. It doesn't stay gentle. Bugs get about 13% tougher
every wave, a stag beetle leads every tenth wave, and from wave 15 the
numbers climb even faster. Here's what we've learned from playing it (and
from a test program that plays it thousands of times).

## Build where the trail bends

The trail snakes down the screen in long rows. A defender standing on the
grass **between two rows** can reach both of them, so every bug walks past it
twice. The tiles right next to a corner are even better: the trail wraps
around them on two sides.

The worst spots are the outer edges of the map, where a defender only
touches one stretch of trail.

## Your first 70 coins

You start with 70 coins and 20 eggs. A strong opening:

1. Two **frogs** near the first bend (30 coins).
2. A **toad** between the first two rows (30 coins).
3. Tap **START**, and keep the last 10 coins for an early upgrade.

Frogs are the best value in the game early on. Toads earn their price once
ants start arriving in tight lines, because one mud glob hits the whole
line.

## Upgrade or build?

A test bot that builds nothing but level 1 frogs stalls around wave 11. One
that **mixes all four defenders and upgrades often** clears about 30 waves
with the same coins. Level 3 defenders hit about three times as
hard as level 1, for a little more than three times the price, and they
don't need a new spot on the map.

A good rhythm: build a new defender, upgrade your best-placed one, repeat.
Upgrade defenders that sit on bends first.

## Know your bugs

- **Mosquitoes** (from wave 3) fly, so toads ignore them. Make sure frogs or
  herons cover the start of the trail, because mosquitoes are fast.
- **Ladybugs** (from wave 5) have a shell that soaks up 2 damage from every
  hit. A level 1 frog does 2 damage, so it barely tickles them. Herons ignore
  most of the shell, and upgraded lilies sting straight through it.
- **Caterpillars** (from wave 7) burst into two ants. Have a frog or a toad
  just past where they usually pop.
- **Stag beetles** (waves 10, 20, 30...) are bosses with lots of health and
  a thick shell, and each one that reaches the pond takes 5 eggs. Herons are
  built for them: they always peck the toughest bug in reach.

## Lilies make everything else better

A lily doesn't hurt anything at level 1. What it does is slow every bug
around it by 40% (up to 60% at level 3), so every defender nearby gets
more shots. A lily on a bend, surrounded by frogs and toads, is one of the
strongest set-ups in the game. Bosses are slowed too.

## Use the speed button and the early call

Between waves you get 9 seconds to build. If you're ready sooner, tap **NEXT
WAVE**: you get a coin for every second left on the clock. On easy waves,
play at 2x or 3x and save your attention for boss waves.

## The numbers

| Defender | Cost | Level 1 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frog | 15, then 20 and 40 | 2 damage, every half second | 6 damage, 3 times a second |
| Toad | 30, then 35 and 60 | 4 splash damage | 12 splash damage, wider splash |
| Lily | 25, then 25 and 45 | slows bugs 40% | slows 60% and stings |
| Heron | 50, then 60 and 100 | 12 damage, long range | 40 damage, longest range |

Medals: bronze at 8 waves cleared, silver at 15, gold at 25, Pond King at 40.
Our best test bot reaches wave 39, so the Pond King medal is meant to be a
real challenge. Good luck, and keep those eggs safe.
