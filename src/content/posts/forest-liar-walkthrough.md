Forest Liar is built to surprise you the first time. That's the joke. But
every trap in the game follows a fixed rule and does exactly the same thing on
every attempt, so once you know what's coming, every level is fair.

This guide has two parts. First, the rules behind each kind of trap, which is
enough to get through most of the game on your own. Then a level-by-level
walkthrough for when you're stuck. **It's full of spoilers.** If you'd like
to be surprised, play a level first and come back when it beats you.

## How scoring works

Each level gives you a score out of 10,000. You lose 40 points for every
second you take and 300 points for every death, and you always get at least
100 for finishing. So a level cleared in 30 seconds with 2 deaths scores
10,000 − 1,200 − 600 = 8,200.

The game keeps your best score, best time and fewest deaths for each level.
Your total on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard/) is your best scores on every
level added together, so clearing more levels always ranks you higher than
replaying one.

Two things follow from the formula: one death costs as much as 7.5 seconds,
so it's sometimes worth waiting a moment for a trap to finish rather than
rushing it. And once you know a level, speed matters, so replaying early
levels with a clean route is the easiest way to climb the board.

## The rules of the forest

**Pip's controls are forgiving.** You can still jump for a tenth of a second
after running off a ledge, and if you press jump slightly before landing, the
jump happens the instant you touch the ground. Holding jump makes it higher;
tapping makes it short. Most "impossible" jumps just need a full, held jump.

**Death is cheap; restarting is a reset.** When you die, you're back at the
last checkpoint star in under a second. Pressing **R** (or the round arrow on
a phone) restarts the whole level from the beginning. Your death count for the
level carries over, so it's not a way to wipe the slate, but it's handy when
you've lost your rhythm and want a clean run.

Here's what each trap does:

| Trap | What it does | How to beat it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Falling sky rock | Drops onto the spot you're standing on when you get close, then stays there as a block. | Keep moving as you approach. Once it has landed, it's just a step to jump over. |
| Tipping sign | Wobbles, then falls in the direction of travel. | Back off when it wobbles, then pass once it's down. |
| Lying sign | Says one thing from far away and something else close up. | Read signs, then ignore them. Follow the flowers instead. |
| Folding bridge | Planks fold away as soon as you step on them. | Look for another route, usually underneath. |
| Cracked plank | Holds for a moment, then folds. | Never stop on one. Run straight across. |
| Fake ground | Looks solid, drops the moment you stand on it. | Jump over suspicious gaps in the pattern of the ground. |
| Hidden spikes | Spikes pop up from the grass as you pass. | Jump over a patch of grass that looks slightly different. |
| Invisible block | Appears when you jump into it from below. | Bumps your head. Jump lower, or use it as a platform. |
| Bouncy mushroom | Launches you high. | Hold jump as you land on it for the biggest bounce. |
| Nervous mushroom | Sweats, then sinks. | Use it as a stepping stone; don't stand on it. |
| Shy mushroom | Ducks when you come close, then lifts you. | Stand on it while it's hiding and ride it up. |
| Sneezing bush | Says "AH..." and blows you sideways. | Get off narrow pillars before it sneezes, or use the sneeze to cross a gap. |
| Acorn stump | Rattles, then rolls an acorn. | Jump the acorn. The rattle tells you when. |
| Welcome mat | Slides away with you on it. | Ride it, and don't jump unless a sign tells you to. |
| Rolling log | Rolls towards you from ahead. | Jump it. |
| Spikes on wheels | Roll back and forth, or follow you once you pass. | Pass when they roll away; don't hang around after passing a follower. |
| Growing tree | Shoots up, then topples towards you. | Run past quickly, or back right off until it has fallen. |
| Stage tree | Drums, then moves platforms around on the beat. | Watch one full cycle, then go. |
| Hopping mailbox | Jumps away the first time you reach it. | Keep walking: it only hops once. |

## Chapter 1: Friendly Forest

### 1-1 Welcome, Probably

The tutorial level, and it already lies to you. A rock drops near the start
as soon as you step towards the first sign, so don't stop there. Further on,
a short stretch of ground is fake, and a log rolls at you from up
ahead: jump it. The "HELLO!" sign tips over, so wait for it. The grass just
before it hides spikes. A wheeled spike waits past the sign and follows you
once you've passed, so keep moving. At the end, the mailbox hops away the
first time you reach it. Walk on and it stays put.

### 1-2 Mushroom Manners

Bounce on the first mushroom to reach the high ground. On the long drop
across the spike pit, the two nervous mushrooms are stepping stones: land and
jump straight off. After the checkpoint, a tree grows and topples, and a
wheeled spike guards the lower path. The last nervous mushroom before the
mailbox sinks, so don't stand on it, and watch for a log rolling in from the
far right.

### 1-3 Bridge of Trust

"Built by honest beavers." The long wooden bridge at the start folds up under
you. The sign under the bridge mentions stairs below, and it's telling the
truth for once: drop down and take the lower route under the bridge, then
climb back up to the checkpoint. After that, a tree grows across your path,
grass hides spikes, and a wheeled spike rolls back and forth near the
end. Time your pass for when it rolls away.

### 1-4 The Polite Bush

A row of narrow dirt pillars over spikes, with bushes that sneeze you off
them. The rule: **when a bush says "AH...", get off the pillars** or be on
solid ground. Later in the level the tip flips: "a good sneeze can also help."
A bush facing the other way can push you across a gap if you jump at the right
moment.

## Chapter 2: Trickster Grove

### 2-1 The Moving Welcome Mat

The first mat slides you across a long spike pit. Stand on it and let it
carry you all the way across. After the
checkpoint, a sign says the next mat is safe "just don't jump. Seriously."
It's right this time. Ride it under the row of downward spikes overhead
without jumping, or you'll hit them.

### 2-2 Helpful Arrow

Every sign in this level lies. "EXIT THIS WAY! >" is wrong, "BIG JUMP AHEAD.
YOU CAN DO IT!" is wrong (you can't), and the last one changes its story as
you get closer. A note near the start gives you the real rule: **signs lie,
flowers don't. Follow the flowers up.** The flowers lead up a staircase of
planks to a high walkway over the gap. Once you drop back down on the far
side, a follower spike is waiting near the mailbox.

### 2-3 Acorn Delivery

Stumps rattle, then roll acorns towards you. Count the rhythm, because each
stump throws on its own timer, then jump each acorn as it reaches you. There's
fake ground on the way up to the first high platform, and an invisible block
hangs in the air above the early route, so use short jumps there. A tree
grows near the top of the big stone hill, and one last stump fires from just
past the mailbox.

### 2-4 The Fake Finish

"Short level! The mailbox is right there. Promise." The mailbox you can see
is fake. Touching it opens a stone wall nearby, which is your real way
forward. Then cross a row of cracked planks without stopping, use the bouncy
mushroom at the far end to get back up, and climb to the real mailbox at the
top right. The grass right before it hides spikes.

## Chapter 3: The Forest's Last Joke

### 3-1 Double Bluff

Cracked planks again, and this time a long row of them over spikes: run and
don't stop. After the checkpoint, the bridge folds, and there seems to be no
way up the giant stone wall. The sign hints: **"Sometimes falling is the way
up."** Drop down and use the bouncy mushroom below. Near the end, a shy
mushroom hides when you approach. Stand on it while it's hiding and it lifts
you up to the exit.

### 3-2 The Quiet Clearing

More pillars over spikes, but now the bushes sneeze in a rhythm: **short,
short, long.** Wait on solid ground through two quick sneezes, then cross
during the long pause. There's an invisible block above the early pillars, so
keep your jumps low. After the bushes, the usual suspects return: hidden
spikes, a growing tree and a follower spike before the mailbox.

### 3-3 Pip vs. The Stagehand

A tree with a drum moves wooden stage platforms on the beat, over a floor of
spikes. The tip in the level is the whole strategy: **watch one round, then
hop.** Each platform moves between two or more fixed spots, so learn where
each one will be on the next beat and jump just after the drum. There's a
checkpoint on the stone block in the middle. The last stretch has a rock that
falls from the sky and a log, so keep moving once you're back on the ground.

### 3-4 Special Delivery

The final level, and "everything you learned, all at once." It's the longest
level in the game, with three checkpoints. In order, you'll meet: a sign that
says "BYE!" and tips over, a bounce mushroom up to high ground, nervous
mushrooms over spikes, a sneezing bush, cracked planks, a stump, the stage
tree's moving platforms, a fake mailbox that opens a wall, a folding bridge
with a bounce mushroom underneath, fake ground, and a final growing tree in
front of the giant boss tree. Take it one checkpoint at a time. Dying only
sends you back to the last star.

When you deliver the letter, the game shows your stats for the whole run.
Congratulations, the forest has run out of jokes. For now.

## Still stuck?

Every level in Forest Liar has been finished by a test bot that plays the
game with the same controls you have, so every level is possible on a phone
too. If a trap seems to break that rule, please tell us through the
[contact page](/contact/).
