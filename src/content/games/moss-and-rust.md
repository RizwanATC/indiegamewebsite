**Moss & Rust** is a fictional sample entry. It exists to show how a game page
looks when a game has **no** browser version, **no** download link, **no**
release year and **no** official website: the page simply leaves those parts
out instead of showing empty buttons.

In the imagined game, a small knight wakes inside a city of stopped machines
that plants have slowly reclaimed. Rusted lifts become shortcuts once you
restart them, and every area loops back to somewhere you've been before.
