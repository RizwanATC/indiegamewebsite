**Lantern Drift** is a sample game made for this site to show how a playable
browser game looks on INDIE/24. It is not a real commercial release.

You are a single paper lantern released on a still night. Embers drift down
from somewhere above, slowly at first and then faster, and one touch is
enough to put you out. Violet sparks fall too: catch them for a burst of
points.

There's no menu to learn and nothing to unlock. A run lasts a minute or two,
which makes it a good fit for the one-game-a-day idea: play a few rounds,
chase your best score, and move on.

### Things to notice

- Movement has a little momentum, so plan your drift instead of twitching.
- Sparks are worth far more than survival time. Taking a small risk for one is usually right.
- Your best score is saved in your own browser only.
