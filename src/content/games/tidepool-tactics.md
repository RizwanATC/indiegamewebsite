**Tidepool Tactics** is a fictional sample entry for an Android game. There is
deliberately no download link: INDIE/24 only lists downloads that come from
the developer or that we're authorized to host, and a sample game has
neither.

The pretend game is a pocket-sized tactics game where the tide is the clock.
Every few turns the water rises, rearranging the board, so a position that
was safe can turn into open sea.
