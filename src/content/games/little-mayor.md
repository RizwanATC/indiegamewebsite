**Little Mayor** is INDIE/24's fourth home-made game: a small city-building
puzzle for people who like to plan.

You are the new mayor of an empty patch of grass with a Town Hall in the
middle. Every day you get two building cards. Pick one, choose a square and
build it. That ends the day, and your town pays its taxes. After 40 days the
term is over, and the number of people living in your town is your score.

### Everything needs a road

A building only works when it touches a road that leads back to the Town
Hall. Roads cost coins but don't use up a day, so you can lay as many as you
can afford. Just don't box yourself in.

### Houses grow

A house starts with 2 people. It grows with a **park** next door (+2), a
**school** or a **clinic** within two squares (+3 each) and **shops** next
door (+1 each, up to two). Small cottages turn into family houses and then
into apartment blocks. Shops also earn you coins from the houses around them.

Land gets dearer as your town grows, so plan your money as well as your map.
You can build over any square to knock down what's there, for a small fee.

### A new town every day

Everyone gets the same buildings in the same order on the same day (in UTC),
so the leaderboard is fair. Play today's town as often as you like. Tomorrow
brings a different one.

### Medals

Bronze at 50 people, silver at 90, gold at 125, and the Key to the City at
150. Your best town is saved on your device.
