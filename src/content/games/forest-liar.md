**Forest Liar** is INDIE/24's second home-made game: a short comedy
platformer about a tiny courier named Pip, a sealed letter, and the
Whisperwood Forest, which does not want that letter delivered.

The forest cheats, but it cheats fairly. Every trap has a tell, and it does
the same thing every time. You will die a lot, and you're back on your feet
in under a second. The second try is where the fun is.

### Things that are lying to you

- **Signs** say helpful things. Then they change their story, or fall on you.
- **Bridges** fold up when you walk onto them. Cracked planks hold for a
  moment, so keep running.
- **Mushrooms**: the bouncy ones launch you, the nervous ones sweat and
  sink, and the shy ones duck when you come close.
- **Bushes** go "AH..." and then sneeze you off ledges.
- **Tree stumps** rattle, then roll acorns at you.
- **Welcome mats** slide away once you step on them.
- **A theatrical tree** moves the scenery around to the beat of its drum.
- **Rocks fall out of the sky** right where you're standing. Watch for the
  shadow, and move.
- **Spikes on wheels** roll back and forth, and some quietly follow you once
  you've walked past.
- **Trees** have growth spurts. If one rumbles, run past it before it falls
  on you.
- **The mailbox** at the end is real. Usually.

### Levels

Twelve levels in three chapters: Friendly Forest, Trickster Grove and The
Forest's Last Joke, with checkpoints on the longer ones. Your unlocked
levels, best times and death counts are saved on your device.

### Phones and tablets

The game shows a touch pad (a d-pad and a jump button) on touch screens,
and every level can be finished with it. Turn your phone sideways for the
best view. On a computer you can switch the touch pad on under Settings.
