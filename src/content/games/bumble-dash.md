**Bumble Dash** is INDIE/24's third home-made game: a one-button flyer
about a small, round bumblebee who really should have taken the long way
round the bramble patch.

Every tap is a flap. Let go and she drops. Thread her through the gaps in
the thorny vines, one gap at a time, for as long as you can. One touch of a
thorn and the run is over, and the next one starts straight away.

### The garden fights back

- **Twisted brambles** stand in pairs with a gap between them. The gaps
  get tighter the further you fly.
- **Honey drops** float between the vines. Grab one for a bonus point, if
  you dare to leave the safe line.
- **Swaying vines** start to bob up and down after a while.
- **Wasps** move in later and hover right where you want to go.
- **Squeezing vines** look wide open from far away, then close in as you
  get there. Nobody warns you about those.

### Four times of day

The garden changes as your score climbs: a sunny meadow, then sunset at
10, a firefly-lit night at 25 and a thunderstorm at 50, each with its own
chiptune song.

### Medals

Bronze at 10, silver at 25, gold at 50, and the Royal Bee medal at 100.
Your best score is saved on your device.
