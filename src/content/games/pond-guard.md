**Pond Guard** is INDIE/24's fourth home-made game: a small tower defence
game that fits in one hand. A clutch of frog eggs is sitting in the pond, and
every bug in the meadow is marching down the trail to get them.

Tap the grass beside the trail to build a defender. Each bug you stop pays a
few coins, and each wave you clear pays a bonus. Spend them on more
defenders, or upgrade the ones you have. Every bug that reaches the pond
takes eggs with it. Lose all 20 eggs and the game is over.

### The defenders

- **Frog** (15 coins): cheap and quick. Flicks its tongue at the bug nearest
  the pond. Good everywhere, great in bunches.
- **Toad** (30 coins): spits a glob of mud that splashes every bug where it
  lands. Can't reach flying bugs.
- **Lily** (25 coins): no attack at first, but its pollen slows every bug
  nearby. Upgraded lilies sting too, and the sting cuts through shells.
- **Heron** (50 coins): a long, slow, heavy peck at the toughest bug in reach.
  Your answer to beetles.

Every defender can be upgraded twice: a flower for level 2, a gold crown for
level 3. Selling gives back 70% of what you spent.

### The bugs

- **Ants** come in long lines from wave 1.
- **Mosquitoes** fly over everything from wave 3. They're fast and frail, and
  toads can't hit them.
- **Ladybugs** wear a shell from wave 5. Small hits barely scratch them.
- **Caterpillars** turn up at wave 7 and burst into two ants when they pop.
- **Stag beetles** lead every 10th wave. They're huge, armoured and slow, and
  each one that gets through takes 5 eggs.

The waves never end and the bugs get tougher every wave, so the question is
how long your pond can hold.

### Medals

Bronze for clearing 8 waves, silver at 15, gold at 25, and the Pond King
medal at 40. Your best score and best wave are saved on your device, and your
best score goes on the leaderboard below.
