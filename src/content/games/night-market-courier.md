**Night Market Courier** is a fictional sample entry.

Picture a game about one evening's worth of deliveries. Each package comes
with a small story attached, and the people you meet start to recognise you
as the night goes on. There's no fail state, only a route to choose and
conversations to finish before the stalls close.
