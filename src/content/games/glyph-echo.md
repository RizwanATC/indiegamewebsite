**Glyph Echo** is a sample game made for this site. It is not a real
commercial release.

The idea is old and simple: the glyphs play a sequence, and you play it back.
Each successful round adds one more step, and the sequence plays a little
faster as it grows. Every glyph has its own colour, shape and tone, so you can
remember it whichever way works for you.

It's included here to show a second kind of browser game on INDIE/24, one
that works equally well with a keyboard, a mouse or a finger.
