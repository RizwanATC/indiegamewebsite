**Signal Strike** is INDIE/24's own first game, made as a love letter to the
side-scrolling shooters that lived on old monochrome mobile phones. It's an
original game written from scratch for this site, and you can play it right
here.

Your ship holds the left of the screen while waves of drones, darts, gunships
and floating mines pour in from the right. Survive a stage and its boss shows
up: a big armoured ship with a health bar, its own attack patterns and a habit
of launching extra drones at you.

### Weapons

Your blaster fires as fast as you can hold the button. On top of that you carry
one **special weapon** with limited ammo, shown at the top of the screen:

- **Missiles** home in on the nearest target and hit hard.
- **Beam** burns a straight line across the whole screen for a moment.
- **Wall** sends a slow barrier forward that soaks up enemy bullets.

Destroyed enemies sometimes drop a crate. Fly into it to switch to that special
and top up its ammo. A rare heart crate gives you an extra life.

### Stages

1. **Outer Rim**: drones and darts, then the first gunship boss.
2. **Rock Belt**: asteroids and mines that fire in three directions.
3. **The Core**: everything at once, and the toughest boss.

Clear all three and the run loops with faster, tougher enemies. Your high
score is saved on your device.
