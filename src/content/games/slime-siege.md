**Slime Siege** is INDIE/24's first home-made game, built to be played
upright like a phone game. It's an original game written for this
site, with all of its pixel art drawn from scratch too.

Your hero marches up a forest path while slimes hop down towards you and
skeletons stop to throw bones. Your sword sends out slashes automatically, so
on a phone you just drag a thumb anywhere on the screen to dodge and aim.

### Things on the path

- **Chests** take three hits to open and drop something useful: a carrot heals
  you, gold is worth points, a crystal gives you a triple slash, and boots make
  you attack faster for a while.
- **Bloom flowers** add a bloom bomb. Set one off to wipe every enemy shot off
  the screen and hurt everything in sight. You start each stage with one.
- **Farm animals** wander across. Walk into one to send it home safely for
  bonus points, but don't hit it with your sword.

### Stages

1. **Green Meadow**: slimes and a few skeletons, then the King Slime.
2. **Old Farm**: more skeletons, then the giant Bone Lord.
3. **Haunted Wood**: big slimes join in, and the King Slime returns angrier.

Clear all three and the run loops with tougher enemies. Your best score is
saved on your device.
