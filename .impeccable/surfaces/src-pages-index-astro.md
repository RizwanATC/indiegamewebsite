## Direction contract

THESIS: INDIE/24 is a capsule-toy machine that dispenses one game a day. Refuses the category default of a near-black page with a neon accent and a split hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Gacha-machine red bodywork owns whole bands, on a cool capsule-white ground. Clear dome windows with a pale glass sheen hold game art. Capsule colours (sunflower, sky, mint, bubblegum) mark genres. Deep navy ink. Heavy toy-packaging display face (Dela Gothic One) over a plain Japanese-gothic workhorse (Zen Kaku Gothic New). Two-tone capsule pills are the control and chip shape.

STORY: The visitor sees today's capsule in the machine, understands one game drops each day, presses the big knob to play, and finds yesterday's capsules lined up below.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A red machine body fills the width. Its dome window holds today's cover art. Its label panel carries a date sticker, the title in heavy display type, one line of description, and the knob-shaped Play Now button with Download and Details beside it. On phones the dome sits above the panel and Play Now is reachable without scrolling.
